Platform privacy

How we handle the data of people who sign up and use the Brigaplan platform: account, projects, uploaded floor plans and the conversation with the assistant. If you only visit the site, the site privacy notice applies.

What data we process

  • Account: email, name, account type (private or professional) and profile, country, city and language. For professionals, also company name and VAT number. We also keep the date and version of the privacy notice we showed you and the date of your last login.
  • Password: we only store it encrypted (Argon2), so not even we can read it.
  • Sign in with Google: if you choose "Sign in with Google", Google sends us your name, your email and a code that identifies your Google account. We do not receive your Google password.
  • Projects: project name, type of venue, city, covers, what you ask us for, the floor plans and files you upload and the declarations you make about the problems flagged by the engine (choice, text, place and date).
  • Your account rules: the choices you ask the assistant to remember. We use them only for your projects: we do not use them for other users or to improve Brigaplan, and no one else sees them.
  • Conversation with the assistant: the messages you write to the assistant, its replies and the previews it prepares (floor plan, 3D, checks).
  • Full design request: the level you chose, your notes and, if you give it, your phone number.
  • Quote requests: which dealers you sent the project to and when, the quotes you receive and the one you accept.
  • Payments and invoices: what you bought, the amount, the date and outcome of the payment, the code Stripe uses to identify it and the invoicing details (name or company name, address, Italian tax code or VAT number, recipient code or certified email address). We never see the card number: only Stripe handles it. If you withdraw, we also keep your withdrawal with its date and time.
  • Support: the messages you write in the “Help” chat, the replies from the AI assistant and the operator, and the email address where you want the reply.
  • Connected apps (for example Claude): which app you connected, the dates and the access codes, stored only as a fingerprint that cannot be read back. See Brigaplan inside Claude.
  • Technical data: to limit login attempts we use your IP address only in memory, for a few minutes, without saving it. The service that hosts the platform records the technical data of visits as described in the site privacy notice.

Floor plans may contain other people's data, for example the designer's name in the title block. Only upload them if you have the right to do so. We use them for your project and never show them to other users.

If you are a professional and upload floor plans or data of your clients, we are an independent controller for that data and process it as described in this notice. You must have the right to upload it and you must inform your clients, for example like this: “To prepare the design I use Brigaplan, a service of Italfrigo Service S.r.l.: how it handles data is explained at brigaplan.ai/privacy-piattaforma.html”. Your clients can exercise their rights directly with us by writing to info@brigaplan.ai.

Why, and on what legal basis

  • Providing the service (account, projects, previews, confirmation and notification emails): this is needed to perform the contract you accept when you sign up (Article 6(1)(b) of the Regulation). Without email, password and account type we cannot open the account.
  • Protecting the platform from abuse and unauthorised access: the controller's legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f)).
  • Sending the project to the dealers you choose, for a quote: your consent, given with the tick box for each request (Article 6(1)(a)). See Quote requests to dealers.
  • Improving Brigaplan (rules, typical equipment, statistics, quality checks and internal examples), using projects without name, company, address and contact details: the controller's legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f)). You can object at any time: see How we use projects to improve Brigaplan. For professional accounts, only if you switch it on.
  • Sending the project to a partner installer, if you ask for it with the checkbox on the project page: your consent (Article 6(1)(a)). You can withdraw it at any time: see Partner installers.
  • Answering support requests: for registered users it is needed to perform the contract (Article 6(1)(b)); for people who are not registered, our legitimate interest in replying (Article 6(1)(f)).
  • Invitation to leave a review on Trustpilot, after a paid service has been delivered: our legitimate interest in collecting customers' opinions (Article 6(1)(f)), under the rules of Article 130(4) of the Italian Privacy Code. You can say no when you buy, from your account or with the link in every invitation.
  • Complying with the law, for example with requests from the authorities or with the tax obligations of payments, such as electronic invoicing: legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c)).

We do not use your data for advertising, we do not sell it and we do not send you newsletters without a separate consent.

The AI assistant and verification by a designer

The preview is prepared by an artificial intelligence assistant (Claude models by Anthropic) together with our design engine, which checks dimensions and rules.

The engine and the AI prepare the preview, the design and the construction drawings. Unless you ask for a designer's check (paid service), the full design or our help, no one on our staff opens your project, except for the sample quality check of previews (never for professional accounts), without your profile data, described in How we use projects to improve Brigaplan. If you ask for the check, or buy the full design when the version has problems, a designer from our staff sees the project, the uploaded files and the conversation with the assistant and, if needed, corrects the work. Our staff also open a project when the law requires it or to protect the service from abuse, and every opening is logged.

The engine's work is indicative and makes no decisions with legal effects on you: Brigaplan gives you a functional equipment layout, which is approved by the qualified professional and consultants you choose.

How we use projects to improve Brigaplan

To make the assistant and the engine work better, we also use the projects made on the platform: to check quality, write and correct the rules, calculate statistics and typical equipment (for example which equipment an 80-seat pizzeria usually needs) and keep internal examples to design similar cases better.

Sample check. To check quality, someone from our staff looks at a sample of previews, in read-only mode: at most 3 projects selected per day. Projects of professional accounts never enter the sample. The person checking sees the preview versions (floor plan, 3D and engine checks), the files you uploaded, as you uploaded them and with their file names, and the venue data the rules need: type of venue, city, seats and energy. They do not see your profile data (name, company, VAT number, address, email and phone number), the project name, your requests or the conversation with the assistant. However, the name of the venue and other details, such as names and addresses, may appear in the files you uploaded and in their names. We log who looked and when; if they download a file to look at it more closely, that is logged too, and we do not keep copies. To show less, upload the floor plan without the title block and with a neutral file name; to show nothing, object from your account: your previews are never selected.

  • Without your data. For this use we remove name, company, address and contact details from the project.
  • Nobody else sees your floor plans. The floor plans and files you upload, that is your venue, and your data are never shown to other users. To other clients we may propose, without any reference to you, only layout solutions prepared by Brigaplan (how zones, counters and equipment are arranged), adapted to their venue.
  • Legal basis: our legitimate interest in improving the service (Article 6(1)(f) of the Regulation).
  • You can object at any time, without giving reasons: in your account there is the option "Do not use my projects to improve Brigaplan", or write to info@brigaplan.ai. From then on we no longer use your projects for this purpose and we remove their copies without names; only figures already aggregated (statistics and rules) remain, which no longer concern you.
  • Professional accounts: the opposite applies. The "Use my projects to improve Brigaplan" box starts switched off: we do not use your projects for this purpose until you switch it on, and you can switch it off whenever you want.

We do not use your projects to train artificial intelligence models, and the AI provider may not do so either.

Brigaplan inside Claude

You can also use Brigaplan from Claude, Anthropic's assistant, by connecting the Brigaplan connector to your Claude account. In that case:

  • Access. You sign in with your Brigaplan account and authorise Claude to see and create your projects. You can revoke the authorisation at any time from the “Connected apps” section of your account. If you change or reset your password, connected apps are disconnected straight away.
  • What we receive. Only what Claude sends us for the project: the details of the venue and the answers to our assistant's questions, sometimes summarised by Claude. We store them like the messages written on the site. We never see the rest of your conversation with Claude. Floor plans can only be uploaded from our own page, not from the chat.
  • What we send to Claude. Our assistant's questions, the status of the project, a short description without brands, dimensions or codes, the price range and links to the site. The floor plan and the 3D are shown in a window inside the chat, reduced and watermarked as on the site, through links that are valid for 10 minutes.
  • Who is the controller. Anthropic is the controller of your conversation with Claude, under its privacy policy. We are the controller of the data that reaches Brigaplan, as for the rest of the platform, in order to provide the service (Article 6(1)(b) of the Regulation).
  • App access. For the connected app we store its name, its return addresses and the date it was connected. We store access codes only as a fingerprint that cannot be read back, with their expiry, last use and revocation: an access code is valid for 1 hour, a renewal code for 30 days, and after 90 days at most Claude has to be connected again.
  • In the project we note that it was created from Claude, for statistics.

Support

In the “Help” chat the AI assistant replies first, using the same provider as the preview. If you ask to speak to a person, the conversation passes to an operator from our staff, who replies in the chat and by email. To alert the operator we use Telegram, which receives only a notice without your data, with the request number: the messages stay on the platform. We keep support messages for 12 months.

Who processes the data for us

We use these providers as processors, under a contract (DPA) that requires them to use the data only on our behalf:

  • DigitalOcean, LLC (United States): servers, database and files of the platform, in the Frankfurt region (Germany). For backups and for the network that delivers the pages (Cloudflare), DigitalOcean also uses providers in the United States. DigitalOcean participates in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.
  • DigitalOcean, LLC, for realistic renders (Toronto data centre, Canada): when you ask for realistic renders, a machine with a GPU is created for the job and destroyed at the end; the floor plan and the 3D model stay there only for the time of the render. The transfer to Canada is based on the European Commission's adequacy decision for Canada.
  • Anthropic Ireland, Limited (Dublin, Ireland): provides the Claude artificial intelligence models. It receives the messages of your conversation with the assistant and the project data and images needed to prepare the preview. The data are also processed in the United States, under the standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission. Anthropic deletes these data within 30 days; if its automated checks flag a breach of its usage policies, it may keep them for up to 2 years. It may not use them to train its models.
  • Brevo SAS (Paris, France): sends the automatic emails (email confirmation, notifications, replies), with servers in France and Belgium. Some of its providers are in the United States or India, under the Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.
  • Zoho Corporation B.V. (Utrecht, Netherlands): manages the info@brigaplan.ai mailbox, with servers in the European Union. Zoho staff in India may access it for technical support, under standard contractual clauses.
  • Stripe Payments Europe, Limited (Ireland), for payments: only Stripe handles card details. To prevent fraud and to meet its own legal obligations, such as anti-money laundering, Stripe uses some data as an independent controller, as described in its privacy policy. For data that leaves the European Union it relies on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and the standard contractual clauses.
  • Trustpilot A/S (Copenhagen, Denmark): after a paid service has been delivered, it sends you the invitation to leave a review, unless you have said no. It receives a blind copy of the order confirmation email, without the files or the links to download them, and takes from it your name, your email address and the order number. If you write a review, Trustpilot's terms and privacy notice also apply.
  • Electronic invoices: they are issued by Italfrigo Service S.r.l. with its own electronic invoicing system and sent to the Italian Exchange System (Sistema di interscambio).

We also process data on one of our own computers, in our office in Milan: that is where the assistant prepares the previews. The computer receives floor plans, conversations and projects from the site and keeps them as long as the account is open.

Sign in with Google. If you choose "Sign in with Google", Google confirms who you are and passes us your name, email and a code that identifies your account. For this part, Google processes the data as an independent controller, under its own privacy policy.

Transfers outside the European Union. For each provider we have indicated the safeguard above: the Data Privacy Framework (European Commission decision of 10 July 2023) or standard contractual clauses. You can ask us for a copy of the safeguards by writing to info@brigaplan.ai.

Render links. If you share your renders with a link, whoever receives it sees only the renders, not the project, the files or your data. The link expires after 30 days and you can switch it off earlier, whenever you want.

Partner installers

If you ask us to with the checkbox on the project page, we send the project and your contact details to the partner installer in your area, so they can give you a quote. In Lombardy the partner is Italfrigo Service S.r.l., the same company that runs Brigaplan: your data go to Italfrigo's sales office, which uses them only to prepare your offer and to look after you if you buy. Other partners, when there are any, will receive them as independent controllers and will use them only for your request. We do this only with your consent, which you give with the checkbox (Article 6(1)(a) of the Regulation). The checkbox is never ticked in advance: if you do not tick it, the project stays with Brigaplan only. You can withdraw your consent at any time from your account, in the “Your consents” section, or by writing to info@brigaplan.ai: from then on we send no further data and we ask those who have already received it to delete it.

We share your project with other installers, dealers or partners only if you ask us to, and only with the ones you choose.

Quote requests to dealers. If on the project page you ask for quotes and tick the box for that request, we send to the dealers you choose (at most 3, all checked by our staff) the project name, the type of venue, the city, the number of covers, the description, the floor plan and the 3D of the version you send, and your name. They do not receive your email or your phone number. Each dealer uses this data only to prepare your quote and is an independent controller of it. If you accept their quote, we send them your email so that they can contact you. If you reject it, or the request expires after 14 days, they can no longer see the floor plan and the 3D. Dealers do not pay Brigaplan to receive your request: you choose who to send it to.

Manufacturer channels. If you come to Brigaplan through the link of an equipment manufacturer, your account is linked to its channel and our designs give priority to its products. If you ask for a quote on the project page and tick the checkbox, we send the project (floor plan, 3D, description) and your contact details to that manufacturer and to its dealers in your area, who use them only to prepare your offer and are independent controllers for them. We do this only with your consent (Article 6(1)(a) of the Regulation), which you can withdraw at any time from your account, in the “Your consents” section, or by writing to info@brigaplan.ai: from then on we send no further data and we ask those who have already received it to delete it. For data already sent you can also contact the manufacturer.

Email

Automatic emails are sent by Brevo. Like many sending services, Brevo puts a small image in emails that shows whether the email has been opened. We do not use this information for advertising or to profile you. If you prefer, set your email program not to load images: the emails remain readable.

For how long

  • Account, projects, files and conversations: as long as the account is open. When you close the account, we immediately delete the account, projects, files and conversations from the platform and from the office computer where we prepare the previews. They remain only in the backups, which delete themselves within 14 days. The AI provider deletes the data it has received according to its own rules, normally within 30 days.
  • Copies of projects without names, to improve Brigaplan: as long as the account is open and you do not object; then we remove them. Figures already aggregated (statistics and rules) remain, because they no longer concern you.
  • Your account rules: for as long as you keep the account. You can export and delete them whenever you want from the "My rules" page; when you close the account they are deleted with it.
  • Automatic emails already sent: we keep them for 90 days, to check that they were delivered.
  • Connected apps: we delete expired or revoked access codes after 30 days and apps without codes after 90 days; everything is deleted when you close your account.
  • Invitations: we keep the email address of the people we invite until they sign up or, if they do not sign up, until 90 days after the invitation expires.
  • Accounts never confirmed: we delete them after 30 days.
  • Inactive accounts: after 24 months without logins we write to you; if you do not reply within 30 days, we delete the account.
  • Shared render links: 30 days, or less if you switch them off earlier.
  • Quote requests and quotes received: with the project, for as long as you keep the account. The dealer can see the floor plan and the 3D only while the request is open; what they keep (project name, city and their quote) they process as an independent controller.
  • Paid designs: if you close your account, we keep a locked copy of the delivered design (accepted offer, drawings, final version and declarations about problems) for 10 years, which we use only to defend ourselves in case of disputes or to meet legal obligations (Article 17(3)(e) of the Regulation). Then we delete it.
  • Support messages: 12 months.
  • Data the law requires us to keep, such as invoices: for the period required by law, usually 10 years.

Cookies

The platform only uses two technical cookies, needed for it to work, so no consent is required:

  • bp_sessione: keeps you logged in to your account and protects forms from forged submissions; it is also created before you log in. It lasts 30 days.
  • lingua: remembers the language you chose. It lasts 1 year.

No analytics, advertising or profiling cookies.

How we protect the data

Encrypted connection (HTTPS), encrypted passwords, files and floor plans visible only to you and to authorised staff, when you ask for a check, the full design or our help and for the sample quality check, a limit on login attempts, and other sessions closed when you change your password.

Your rights

You can ask at any time to access your data, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict its processing or to object to it, and to receive it in a readable format (portability), as provided for by Articles 15 to 22 of the Regulation. You can do this from your dashboard or by writing to info@brigaplan.ai. We reply within one month.

In the “Download your data” file in your account you will find everything you uploaded, complete and in its original form. The free previews prepared by Brigaplan (floor plan and 3D) are included as you see them on the site: the floor plan reduced and the 3D with the words “not valid for quotes or for the building site”. With the full design you get them complete.

If you think the processing is not lawful, you can lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (garanteprivacy.it).

Other things to know

  • The platform is for people aged 18 or over: industry professionals, people opening or renovating a venue, and private individuals too.
  • If we change this notice in a significant way, we will email you first. The version you read stays recorded in your account.
