How it works

From floor plan to preview, in a few minutes.

Upload the floor plan or describe the space, tell us what you want and get a free preview with floor plan, 3D and cost estimate. A designer checks it before you see it.

The steps

  1. 1Upload the floor plan or describe the spaceA DXF or DWG file works, or a PDF. No floor plan? We ask you for dimensions, doors, windows and where the dining room is.
  2. 2Tell us what you needType of venue, covers, menu, and which of your existing equipment you want to keep.
  3. 3Get your previewFloor plan with zones and counters, 3D you can rotate with a finger, cost estimate. In a few minutes.
  4. 4Go ahead only if you like itDimensioned design, construction drawings signed by a designer, installation by the partner installer in your area.

Five examples

Each video starts from the drawing and ends with the design in stylized 3D, as you see it on the platform. Drawing and 3D come out of our design engine, with equipment sizes taken from the manufacturers' data sheets.

In the free preview the 3D model is simplified and the equipment is generic, with no brands. Photorealistic renders are a separate service, on request.

More previews

Six previews prepared by our assistant on test cases and checked by the engine. They are like the ones you get for free: floor plan without dimensions and simplified 3D, with generic equipment.
