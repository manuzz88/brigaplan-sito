How it works

From the floor plan to the preview, checked by the engine.

You upload the floor plan or describe the venue, say what you want to achieve and get a free preview with floor plan, 3D and a cost estimate for the equipment only, without transport and installation. The AI makes the draft and the engine, our automatic rule check, checks it. If you want, a designer can also check it (paid service).

The steps

  1. 1Upload the floor plan or describe the spaceA DXF or DWG file works, or a PDF. No floor plan? We ask you for dimensions, doors, windows and where the dining room is.
  2. 2Tell us what you needType of venue, covers, menu, and which of your existing equipment you want to keep.
  3. 3Get your previewFloor plan with zones and counters, 3D you can rotate with your finger, cost estimate for the equipment only. The AI makes the draft and the engine checks it against the rules. If you want, a designer can also check it (paid service).
  4. 4Go ahead only if you like itFull design and construction drawings, paid online, and installation by the partner installer in your area.

Five examples

Each video starts from the drawing and ends with the design in stylized 3D, as you see it on the platform. Drawing and 3D come out of our design engine, with the manufacturers' equipment dimensions.

In the free preview the 3D model is simplified and the equipment is generic, with no brands. Photorealistic renders are a separate service, on request.

Excluding VAT: design €19 + €0.50 per m², minimum €29 · construction drawings €69 + €1.50 per m², minimum €99 · check €24 + €0.25 per m², minimum €29. Prices including VAT are rounded to the nearest euro.

The m² are those of the work areas: kitchen, preparation, washing, storerooms, cold rooms and bar counter. Above 200 m², canteens and hospitals: on quotation. You see the exact price on your preview before you buy. If you choose a version with wall changes, the price is calculated on the m² of the work areas of that version, that is after the changes.

How you pay. You buy the full design and the construction drawings from the project page and pay online by card; the designer's check can also be paid with credits. For very large venues the price is by quote.

If you buy the equipment from a partner installer, the cost of the design is deducted from the order and the construction drawings are included in the order. This applies to end customers, not to professionals.
