FAQ

Does Brigaplan replace the designer?

No. Brigaplan gives you a functional equipment layout and has it checked by a rules engine. Site survey, permits and compliance of the premises stay with your technician; designers and dealers can use it to work faster.

Is there an alternative to CAD software for designing a commercial kitchen?

Yes, if you need a layout with the equipment and do not want to draw it yourself: upload the floor plan or describe the premises and get a free preview. If you already work in AutoCAD or Revit, Brigaplan's construction drawings also come as DXF.

What software can I use to design a commercial kitchen if I am not a technician?

CAD software is made for designers and dealers. Brigaplan is designed for restaurant owners too: you do not draw, you answer a few questions.
