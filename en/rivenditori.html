Your projects and clients stay yours

Your projects and your clients are yours. No partner installer, not even Italfrigo as an installer, receives your projects or your clients' contact details. Unless you ask for a check, the full design or our help, no one on our staff opens your projects: they are processed by the engine and the AI. Projects of professional accounts never enter the sample quality check. Anyone on our staff who carries out the check, fixes the full design or helps you is bound by written confidentiality and passes nothing on to Italfrigo's sales business.

Your brand

With your brand active, the full design and the construction drawings carry your logo and your letterhead (company name, address, VAT number and contact details) in the title block, in the PDFs, in the installation layout and on the 3D page. You also choose your preferred brands. The free preview stays with the Brigaplan brand. The brand is included in the subscription, with the line “made with Brigaplan”. For now we activate it ourselves, on request: write to us.

Subscriptions for professionals

Monthly subscriptions for dealers and installers with a VAT number:

  • One area: €39 + VAT a month (€47.58 including VAT), with 60 credits included every month.
  • Several areas: €79 + VAT a month (€96.38 including VAT), with 150 credits included every month.

A new project costs 3 credits and a change 1: with 60 credits you can make, for example, 20 new projects or 60 changes. Included credits do not carry over to the next month and are used before those from packs. With the subscription your brand goes on the full design and the construction drawings. It renews every month; if you cancel it, it lasts until the end of the paid month.

You activate them from your account with online payment, which we are opening soon. If you want to be among the first, write to us.

Your studio rules

Brigaplan learns from you, only for you: your choices become the rules of your account. They do not improve the platform and no one else sees them.

  • How they are made: when you correct a project, the assistant asks you "Do you want me to remember this?". If you say yes, the choice becomes a rule of your account and applies to your next projects, for example how you lay out the dishwashing area or the brands you prefer.
  • Where to find them: on the "My rules" page. You can change, export or delete them whenever you want.
  • The limit: your rules cannot go below the legal minimums that the engine checks, such as the width of passageways and escape routes or the separation between clean and dirty: there the law applies, and the assistant tells you so.

Who sees your floor plans

No one on our staff opens your projects unless you ask for a check, the full design or our help: they are processed by the engine and the AI. Projects of professional accounts never enter the sample quality check.

  • Who sees them: you. Someone from our staff only if you ask for a check, the full design or our help, and every opening is logged; whoever opens them is bound by written confidentiality and passes nothing on to Italfrigo's sales business. We never show them to other users and we do not give them to a partner unless you ask us to.
  • Where they are kept: on the platform's servers, in Germany. To prepare the preview the AI provider also receives them; it also processes them in the United States under the standard contractual clauses, works only on our behalf and may not use them to train its models. If you ask for realistic renders, the floor plan and the 3D model go to a machine with a GPU in Canada, which exists only for the time of the render.
  • How long they stay: as long as you keep your account. When you close it we delete them straight away; in backup copies they remain for 14 days at most. For paid projects we keep a closed copy for 10 years.
  • How to delete them: close your account from the dashboard whenever you want, or write to info@brigaplan.ai. We do not use them to improve the service unless you choose to from your account: for professionals the box starts switched off.

All the details are in the platform privacy notice.

What the engine checks

Over 27,000 pieces of equipment from more than 80 brands, with dimensions taken from the manufacturers' catalogues and data sheets, and more than 40 rules measured automatically on every version, including:

  • Separate routes for clean and dirty, from goods delivery to the dining room.
  • Width of work aisles and escape routes.
  • Hoods according to EN 16282, with the right overhang in front of ovens and fryers.
  • Hand basins, sinks, waste area and changing rooms, according to the rules of the European Union, Italy and Lombardy.
  • Equipment dimensions and connections taken from the manufacturers' data sheets, never made up.

The professional account

  • You sign up choosing the professional profile, with company name and VAT number.
  • You get a dashboard with your clients' projects, versions and files.
  • You have 2 projects with the free preview, each with up to 3 versions; if you need more, write to us. You request the full design and the construction drawings from the project page: you receive the offer and pay by bank transfer.
  • Your price lists: upload your price lists with your discounts. Only you can see them, not even the staff who check your projects, and they are used to make accurate quotes.
  • Your machines: if a piece of equipment is missing, you upload it with its technical data sheet, and a photo if you like; we read it and check it automatically. If it passes the checks it becomes a 2D and 3D block for everyone, if not it stays in your projects.
  • Quote requests: receive requests from restaurateurs in your area, free of charge, if you are a verified dealer. Switch on "I receive requests" in your account: our staff checks that your business is in the sector.

What Brigaplan does and does not do

Brigaplan gives you a functional equipment layout. For health authority (ASL), HACCP, fire safety (Vigili del fuoco) and building permits you need a design signed by a qualified professional of your choice, and the layout must be approved by your consultants. Before work starts on site, the installer checks dimensions and connections.
