Your projects and clients stay yours

Your projects and your clients are yours. No dealer or installer receives your projects or your clients' contact details, unless you send them yourself. Unless you ask for our help, no one on our staff opens your projects: they are processed by the engine and the AI. Projects of professional accounts never enter the sample quality check. Anyone on our staff who helps you is bound by confidentiality and passes nothing on to any sales business.

Your brand

With your brand active, the all-in design carries your logo and your letterhead (company name, address, VAT number and contact details) in the title block, in the PDFs, in the installation layout and on the 3D page. There is also the line “made with Brigaplan”. The free preview stays with the Brigaplan brand. The brand is included in the subscription, which you switch on in “My account” › “Your plan”.

Subscriptions for professionals

Monthly subscriptions for dealers and installers with a VAT number: one area €39 + VAT a month (€47.58 including VAT) with 60 credits included every month, several areas €79 + VAT a month (€96.38 including VAT) with 150 credits. Included credits do not carry over to the next month; the subscription renews automatically every month and, if you cancel it, it lasts until the end of the paid month. You switch it on in “My account”, in the “Your plan” section, by card.

With credits, up to 150 m² of work areas a new project costs 3 credits and a change 1; over 150 and up to 500 m², 6 credits and 2.

The all-in design. A single product: dimensioned floor plan, legend, full 3D, elevations with the connections, tables for the installer, services plan and DXF and CSV files. Without a subscription you pay for it once, online: €39 including VAT (€31.97 + VAT) up to 150 m² of work areas and €79 including VAT (€64.75 + VAT) from 150 to 200 m², with 3 updates included; from the fourth, €11 including VAT (€9.02 + VAT) each. With an active subscription you pay for it in credits: 10 credits up to 150 m² and 20 from 150 to 200 m², each time you generate or regenerate it, with no updates included. At your first order, if you have a VAT number, you declare with a tick box that you are ordering for your business. Before buying it you see the floor area and the price, and you confirm. Over 200 m², and for canteens, hospitals, catering and banqueting, it is not automatic.

Receive requests from your area

If you sell or install kitchens and bars, switch on "I receive requests" in your account and enter the areas you serve; you need a subscription, a free period with an agent's code or an offered subscription. A restaurant owner can send you their project for a quote: each request goes to at most 3 verified dealers, chosen by them, or to a single one with "Entrust the project" from the list. No area is exclusive.

Public list and manufacturer brands

Public list: if your company is verified, from your profile you can appear in the public list of dealers, with name, head office, areas served and description; no email addresses, phone numbers or names of people. The order is random, changes every day and is the same for everyone: nobody pays to be higher up.

Manufacturer brands: from your account you ask a manufacturer that has joined for access to one of its brands: it can grant you library, data sheets and price list. If you choose to be found by manufacturers in your area, they see your company name and the areas you serve, never the other brands, your clients or your projects.

Your studio rules

Brigaplan learns from you, only for you: your choices become the rules of your account. They do not improve the platform and no one else sees them.

  • How they are made: when you correct a project, the assistant asks you "Do you want me to remember this?". If you say yes, the choice becomes a rule of your account and applies to your next projects, for example how you lay out the dishwashing area or the brands you prefer.
  • Where to find them: on the "My rules" page. You can change, export or delete them whenever you want.
  • The limit: your rules cannot go below the legal minimums that the engine checks, such as the width of passageways and escape routes or the separation between clean and dirty: there the law applies, and the assistant tells you so.

Who sees your floor plans

No one on our staff opens your projects unless you ask for our help, except in the cases set out in the terms (legal obligations, checking a possible abuse of the free trial): they are processed by the engine and the AI. Projects of professional accounts never enter the sample quality check.

  • Who sees them: you. Someone from our staff only if you ask for our help, or in the cases set out in the terms (for example an abuse check or a legal obligation), and every opening is logged; whoever opens them is bound by confidentiality and passes nothing on to any sales business. We never show them to other users and we do not give them to a dealer unless you ask us to.
  • Where they are kept: on the platform's servers, in Germany. To prepare the preview the AI provider also receives them; it also processes them in the United States under the standard contractual clauses, works only on our behalf and may not use them to train its models. When realistic renders are available, if you ask for them the floor plan and the 3D model will go to a machine with a GPU in Canada, which exists only for the time of the render.
  • How long they stay: as long as you keep your account. When you close it we delete them straight away; in backup copies they remain for 14 days at most. For paid projects we keep a closed copy for 10 years.
  • How to delete them: close your account from the dashboard whenever you want, or write to info@brigaplan.ai. We do not use them to improve the service unless you choose to from your account: for professionals the box starts switched off.

All the details are in the platform privacy notice.

What you gain

Dealers and sales agents

Quick proposals for your clients, even with your own logo. Stop giving away hours of design work to people who then don't buy.

Installers

The all-in design, with dimensioned connections, before the work starts and, if you switch on "I receive requests", quote requests from restaurant owners in your area.

What the engine checks

Over 8,800 pieces of equipment from 64 brands, with dimensions confirmed by the manufacturers' data sheets, catalogues and drawings, and more than 40 rules measured automatically on every version, including:

  • Separate routes for clean and dirty, from goods delivery to the dining room.
  • Width of work aisles and escape routes.
  • Hoods according to EN 16282, with the right overhang in front of ovens and fryers.
  • Hand basins, sinks, waste area and changing rooms, according to the rules of the European Union, Italy and Lombardy.
  • Connection type and power ratings from the manufacturer's catalogue; the position comes from the model's drawing where there is one, otherwise it is shown as typical, to be checked on the data sheet.

The professional account

  • You sign up choosing the professional profile, with company name and VAT number.
  • You get a dashboard with your clients' projects, versions and files.
  • The first preview and one change are free, then you continue with credits. Without a subscription, you buy the all-in design from the project page and pay online by card or, where your phone allows it, with Apple Pay or Google Pay.
  • Your price lists: upload your price lists with your discounts. Only you can see them, not even the staff who help you; they are encrypted and used to make accurate quotes.
  • Your machines: if a piece of equipment is missing, you upload it with its technical data sheet, and a photo if you like; we read it and check it automatically. If it passes the checks it becomes a 2D and 3D block for everyone, if not it stays in your projects.
  • Quote requests: if you are a verified dealer or installer, with a subscription you receive requests from restaurateurs in the areas you serve; each client sends them to at most 3 dealers. Switch on "I receive requests" in your account: our staff checks that your business is in the sector.

What Brigaplan does and does not do

Brigaplan gives you a functional equipment layout. For health authority (ASL), HACCP, fire safety (Vigili del fuoco) and building permits you need a design signed by a qualified professional of your choice, and the layout must be approved by your consultants. Before work starts on site, the installer checks dimensions and connections.
