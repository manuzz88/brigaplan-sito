Connector for Claude

Brigaplan inside Claude

With the Brigaplan connector you prepare a free preview of a commercial kitchen or bar by talking to Claude, Anthropic's assistant. The preview is drawn by the Brigaplan engine, which checks it against the rules, just as on the website. If you want, you can also have it checked by a designer from the website (paid service).

What you can do from the chat

  • Create a preview: describe the venue (type, city, seats, square metres) and what you want to achieve.
  • Answer our assistant: before drawing, it asks for what it needs, for example doors, windows, gas or electric.
  • Upload the floor plan: Claude gives you a personal link to a page on our site, valid for 7 days: you sign in with your account and upload up to 5 files. Files in the chat do not reach us.
  • See the preview in a window inside the chat: floor plan with zones and counters, stylized 3D you can rotate and the equipment price range.
  • Open the design on the site, if you want the full design or a quote.

What stays on the site

Full design and construction drawings, quotes, payments, downloads and account settings are only on brigaplan.ai. From the chat you cannot pay for or delete anything.

How to add it

  1. You need a Brigaplan account, which is free. During the trial you also need an invitation code.
  2. In Claude, on the web or in the desktop app, open “Customize” › “Connectors”. If Brigaplan is in the list, add it from there; otherwise click “+ Add” › “Add custom connector” and enter the name Brigaplan and the address https://app.brigaplan.ai/mcp.
  3. Claude opens the Brigaplan sign-in page: you sign in with your account and authorise Claude to see and create your projects.
  4. To use it in a chat, click “+” at the bottom left, then “Connectors”, and switch Brigaplan on. It also works in the Claude app on your phone. In Team and Enterprise accounts the connector is added by an administrator.

You can disconnect Claude at any time from the “Connected apps” section of your Brigaplan account.

Example requests

  • “I’m opening an 80-seat trattoria in Milan. The kitchen is 9 × 5.5 m, with one door to the dining room. Use Brigaplan to make me a preview of the kitchen.”
  • “With Brigaplan, lay out the bar of a 40-seat venue: a 6-metre counter, espresso machine, glass washer and drinks fridge.”
  • “I run a 100-seat pizzeria with a wood-fired oven: use Brigaplan to prepare a preview of the prep kitchen, with the equipment price range.”

Good to know

  • The free previews of your account apply, as on the site: 2 projects, each with up to 3 versions.
  • The preview is indicative: no dimensions, brands or codes. It is not enough to build from. See What a preview is, and what it is not.
  • You see the preview straight away, labelled “AI draft, checked by the rules engine”. If the engine finds problems it points them out: the assistant fixes them free of charge and, if it cannot, offers you a check by a designer, which you order from the website.
  • Brigaplan gives you a functional equipment layout. For health authority (ASL), HACCP, fire safety (Vigili del fuoco) and building permits you need a design signed by a qualified professional of your choice, and the layout must be approved by your consultants. Before work starts on site, the installer checks dimensions and connections.
  • What Claude writes in its own words is not written by us: what counts is what you see in the Brigaplan window and on the site.
  • We work with partner installers by area: in Lombardy it is Italfrigo Service S.r.l., the company that runs Brigaplan. You ask for a quote for equipment and installation only if you want to, from the site, and you are not obliged to buy from a partner.

Your data

We only receive what Claude sends us for the project: the details of the venue and your answers to our assistant. We do not see the rest of your conversation with Claude. Floor plan and 3D are shown in the Brigaplan window, reduced and watermarked. The details are in the platform privacy notice and in the terms of use.
