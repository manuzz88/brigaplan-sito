What the platform offers

  • Free preview: you upload the floor plan or describe the venue and get a floor plan with zones and counters, a simplified 3D and a cost estimate as a range. It is prepared by an artificial intelligence assistant together with our design engine, which checks it against the rules. You see it straight away, labelled “automatic draft, not checked by a designer”.
  • Check by a designer: if you ask for it, someone from our staff checks the preview and, if needed, corrects it. It is optional and paid: first you receive an offer with price, contents and timing, which you accept separately, and you pay by bank transfer.
  • Design and construction drawings: these are paid. Before work starts you receive an offer with price, contents and timing, which you accept separately.
  • Partner installers: if you ask us to, we put you in touch with an installer in your area.

The service has just started: some features may change, be suspended or arrive later.

Your account

  • To sign up you must be an adult and give true information. If you sign up as a professional, the company name and VAT number must be those of your business.
  • You can sign up as a private individual, including for a home kitchen, or as a professional. For all private accounts we apply the protections that the law gives consumers.
  • The account is personal: keep your password private and tell us immediately if you think someone else is using it.
  • One account per person. Do not open more accounts to get more free previews.
  • You can close your account whenever you want, from the dashboard or by writing to us.

Free previews

Each account can create 2 projects with a free preview, each with up to 3 versions. We may change these numbers for new projects, with notice. On the “My projects” page you can see how many you have left. If a preview fails because of a technical error on our side, it does not count, and neither do versions created from instructions by our staff.

What a preview is, and what it is not

  • The preview is indicative: it helps you understand space, footprint and costs. It is not a set of construction drawings and it is not enough to build.
  • The preview is prepared by an artificial intelligence assistant and our design engine: a person sees it only if you ask for a designer's check, for a fee, or for the sample quality check, unless you object (never for professional accounts). The full design and the construction drawings are prepared by a designer, starting from the AI's proposal.
  • Brigaplan gives you a functional equipment layout. For health authority (ASL), HACCP, fire safety (Vigili del fuoco) and building permits you need a design signed by a qualified professional of your choice, and the layout must be approved by your consultants. Before work starts on site, the installer checks dimensions and connections. Brigaplan does not sign code-compliant designs. The construction drawings do not replace the building services design: when the law requires it (Ministerial Decree 37/2008), it is drawn up and signed by a registered professional.
  • An automatic draft has not been seen by a designer: it may contain errors. If the engine finds problems it points them out, and the assistant fixes them free of charge.
  • The cost estimate is an approximate range, not an offer: the real price is set by whoever sells and installs the equipment.
  • Equipment dimensions come from the manufacturers' data sheets, but they must always be checked before ordering.
  • We work with the measurements of the venue that you give us (floor plan, photos, description), without a site visit. For this reason all measurements, of the venue and of the connections, must be checked on site before ordering the equipment and starting the works: we also write this on the drawings.
  • The rules the engine checks are those of the European Union and Italy, including those of Lombardy and Milan. Elsewhere, local rules must be checked with a local professional.

The floor plans and files you upload

  • Only upload files you have the right to use. If you upload a DWG or someone else's drawing, you declare that you have permission.
  • You authorise us to use your files only to provide the service: to prepare and check your project and, if you ask us to, to share it with the partners you choose.
  • If you upload floor plans or data of your clients, you must have the right to do so and you must inform them that you use Brigaplan. For this data Brigaplan is an independent controller: your clients can contact us directly, as explained in the platform privacy notice.
  • We do not publish your files, we do not show them to other users and we do not use them to train artificial intelligence models.
  • We also use projects, without name, company, address and contact details, to improve Brigaplan: rules, statistics and internal examples. To check quality, someone from our staff looks at a sample of previews and uploaded files, without your profile data (name, company, address, contact details); the files you uploaded are shown as you uploaded them. You can object at any time from your account; the details are in the platform privacy notice.
  • Do not upload files with unlawful content, viruses or other people's data that the project does not need.

Partner installers and purchases

  • Brigaplan does not sell equipment. If you buy from a partner installer, the supply and installation contract is between you and them.
  • In Lombardy the partner installer is Italfrigo Service S.r.l., that is, the same company that provides Brigaplan. Other partners pay Brigaplan a commission on sales that come from projects made here.
  • You are not obliged to buy from a partner. If you do, the cost of the design is deducted from your order as stated in the offer.
  • If you come to Brigaplan through the link of an equipment manufacturer, your designs give priority to its products and its logo may appear in the preview: we say so at the top of the page. You always pay Brigaplan for the full design.
