For foodservice equipment manufacturers

Your equipment, with the right dimensions.

Brigaplan designs kitchens and bars with the manufacturers' dimensions, never made up. Here is where your equipment appears and how to keep it up to date.

The catalogue

Over 23,000 pieces of equipment from 80 brands, with dimensions confirmed by the manufacturers' data sheets and drawings. The engine uses them to place counters and machines in the floor plan and to check walkways, hoods and connections.

Where brands appear

  • Not in the free preview: it has generic blocks drawn from the dimensions, with no brands or codes.
  • Yes in the full design: the legend shows the brand and code of every piece of equipment.
  • Professionals with their own branding switched on choose their preferred brands.
  • There are no brand logos in the images.

Your files

We do not publish your CAD files or your logos on the site: the preview and the images use blocks we draw ourselves from the official dimensions.

Is a model missing, or a dimension wrong?

Send us the official data sheet at info@brigaplan.ai: we check it and correct the catalogue.

Professionals can also upload a missing piece of equipment with its data sheet: if it passes the automatic checks, it becomes a 2D and 3D block for everyone.

Who is behind it

Brigaplan is a service of Italfrigo Service S.r.l., which is also a partner installer in Lombardy. Clients remain free to choose who to buy from: they can use projects made with Brigaplan with any supplier they prefer.
