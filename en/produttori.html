For foodservice equipment manufacturers

Bring your brand to Brigaplan.

Brigaplan designs professional kitchens and bars with the dimensions of real equipment. In designs, equipment has no brand: your brand appears only if you sign up and give us your consent.

Why it pays

  • Your brand in designs: once they have been checked, your models appear with brand and code in customers' paid designs: full design, construction drawings and subscribers' projects. The free preview and the renders show no brands.
  • More models: designs include the models you give us, not only those we find in public data sheets.
  • Data from you: dimensions, connections and drawings as you provide them, updated when you send us new ones.
  • Connections from your drawings: in the construction drawings, the position and power of the connections come from your dimensioned drawings, not from typical positions.

How it works

  1. You sign up as a manufacturer: the basic membership is free.
  2. You list your brands and send us your library: data sheets, dimensions, connections and CAD drawings. The manufacturer dashboard is already in your account, for permissions.
  3. You choose the permissions, separately: using the technical data of your products in customers' designs, and showing your brands.
  4. Grant dealers access to your brands: a dealer asks you for access, and you see whether it is verified, or you offer it. You can give them library, data sheets and price list, including to all verified dealers in an area; list prices only to one verified dealer at a time, and the end customer never sees them.
  5. We check the data before it goes into customers' designs.

Sign up as a manufacturer

You give or withdraw the permissions whenever you want from “My account”, in the “Manufacturer permissions” box.

Your files and your data

  • The price list is optional: if you upload it, we store it encrypted. We do not show it to customers or to other manufacturers.
  • Where they are kept: your files are kept in the site database, on DigitalOcean servers in Frankfurt. Brigaplan's work computer, in Italy, opens them in a closed environment with no network access to extract dimensions and data; the resulting catalogue is kept on that computer and on our servers.
  • Artificial intelligence: to read data sheets we also use an artificial intelligence service (Anthropic). It receives the content of the data sheets and may also process it outside the European Union, with the safeguards provided by the GDPR; under its contract it does not use it to train its models.

The catalogue

Brigaplan already knows over 8,800 equipment models from 64 brands.

The engine uses them to place counters and machines in the floor plan and to check clearances, hoods and connections. In designs, the legend gives the type, dimensions, power supply and power rating of every piece of equipment; brand and code for manufacturers who have joined Brigaplan. In the free preview there are generic blocks, without brands or codes, and the images show no logos.

Missing a model, or a dimension that doesn't add up?

Send us the manufacturer's data sheet at info@brigaplan.ai: we check it and correct the catalogue.

Professionals can also upload a missing piece of equipment with its data sheet: if it passes the automatic checks, it becomes a 2D and 3D block for everyone.
