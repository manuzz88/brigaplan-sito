For dealers, designers and architects

The kitchen inside your project, ready for you to refine.

Brigaplan prepares the first kitchen or bar proposal from the floor plan: zones, counters, flows and equipment, with the rules already checked. You refine it with your experience and take it to your client.

Your projects and clients stay yours

Your projects and your clients are yours. No partner installer, not even Italfrigo as an installer, receives your projects or your clients' contact details. Unless you ask for a check, the full design or our help, no one on our staff opens your projects: they are processed by the engine and the AI. Projects of professional accounts never enter the sample quality check.

Who sees your floor plans

No one on our staff opens your projects unless you ask for a check, the full design or our help: they are processed by the engine and the AI. Projects of professional accounts never enter the sample quality check.

  • Who sees them: you. Someone from our staff only if you ask for a check, the full design or our help, and every opening is logged. We never show them to other users and we do not give them to a partner unless you ask us to.
  • Where they are kept: on the platform's servers, in Germany. To prepare the preview the AI provider also receives them; it also processes them in the United States under the standard contractual clauses, works only on our behalf and may not use them to train its models.
  • How long they stay: as long as you keep your account. When you close it we delete them straight away; in backup copies they remain for 14 days at most.
  • How to delete them: close your account from the dashboard whenever you want, or write to info@brigaplan.ai. From your account you can also object to the use of projects to improve the service.

All the details are in the platform privacy notice.

What you gain

Architects and interior designers

The kitchen inside your project, with zones and flows already laid out and checked by the engine. With the construction drawings you get the DXF to put in your own drawings.

Kitchen designers

A first proposal right away, for you to refine with your experience. Fewer hours on each revision, more time for the projects that go ahead.

Dealers and sales agents

Quick proposals for your clients, even with your own logo. Stop giving away hours of design work to people who then don't buy.

What the engine checks

  • Separate routes for clean and dirty, from goods delivery to the dining room.
  • Width of work aisles and escape routes.
  • Hoods according to EN 16282, with the right overhang in front of ovens and fryers.
  • Hand basins, sinks, waste area and changing rooms, according to the rules of the European Union, Italy and Lombardy.
  • Equipment dimensions and connections taken from the manufacturers' data sheets, never made up.

The professional account

  • You sign up as a professional, with company name and VAT number.
  • You get a dashboard with your clients' projects, versions and files.
  • For each project you get the preview; you request the design and construction drawings when you need them, with a separate offer.

Coming soon

  • Quote requests: clients who design on their own can send you the finished project to get your price.
  • A growing library: if a piece of equipment is missing, upload it with its data sheet and photos. After a check it becomes a 2D and 3D block for everyone.

What Brigaplan does and does not do

Brigaplan gives you a functional equipment layout. For health authority (ASL), HACCP, fire safety (Vigili del fuoco) and building permits you need a design signed by a qualified professional of your choice, and the layout must be approved by your consultants. Before work starts on site, the installer checks dimensions and connections.

Who is behind it

Brigaplan is a service of Italfrigo Service S.r.l., which is also a partner installer in Lombardy. Clients remain free to choose who to buy from: they can use projects made with Brigaplan with any supplier they prefer.
