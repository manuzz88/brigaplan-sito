Try it free
For dealers, designers and architects

The kitchen inside your project, in a few minutes.

Brigaplan prepares the first kitchen or bar proposal from the floor plan: zones, counters, flows and equipment, with the rules already checked. You refine it with your experience and take it to your client.

What you gain

Architects and interior designers

The kitchen inside your project, in a few minutes, with zones and flows already set up. With the construction drawings you get the DXF to put into your own drawings.

Kitchen designers

A first proposal right away, for you to refine with your experience. Fewer hours on each revision, more time for the projects that go ahead.

Dealers and sales agents

Quick proposals for your clients, even with your own logo. Stop giving away hours of design work to people who then don't buy.

What the engine checks

  • Separate routes for clean and dirty, from goods delivery to the dining room.
  • Width of work aisles and escape routes.
  • Hoods according to EN 16282, with the right overhang in front of ovens and fryers.
  • Hand basins, sinks, waste area and changing rooms, according to the rules of the European Union, Italy and Lombardy.
  • Equipment dimensions and connections taken from the manufacturers' data sheets, never made up.

The professional account

  • You sign up as a professional, with company name and VAT number.
  • You get a dashboard with your clients' projects, versions and files.
  • For each project you get the preview; you request the design and construction drawings when you need them, with a separate offer.

Coming soon

  • Your manufacturers: choose which brands you work with.
  • Your price lists: upload your price lists with your discounts. Only you can see them, and they are used to make accurate quotes on your projects.
  • Quote requests: clients who design on their own can send you the finished project to get your price.
  • A growing library: if a piece of equipment is missing, upload it with its data sheet and photos. After a check it becomes a 2D and 3D block for everyone.
