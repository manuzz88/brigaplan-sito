BrigaplanBot

BrigaplanBot is the program Brigaplan uses to read the public data sheets and drawings of foodservice equipment manufacturers. It identifies itself with the user agent BrigaplanBot/1.0 (+https://brigaplan.ai/bot).

What it reads

Only data sheets and drawings that manufacturers publish on their official websites, to take the dimensions of the equipment and the position of the utility connections (electricity, water, drainage, gas). It reads only the websites on our own list of manufacturers, checked by hand, over https only: no forms, no logins, no cookies.

What it does not do

It does not collect personal data. It does not read or store prices or price lists. It does not republish your files: we keep only the technical data read, with the address and date of the page, the file fingerprint and a short supporting sentence.

How it behaves

It follows your robots.txt, which it reads again every 24 hours, and the website terms of use: if they forbid automated reading, the website is not read. It makes at most one request every 5 seconds and 200 a day per website; if the website answers 429 or 503, it stops for that day.

How to exclude it

Add these lines to your robots.txt:

User-agent: BrigaplanBot
Disallow: /

Or write to us at info@brigaplan.ai and we will remove your website from the list.
