For installers of commercial kitchen equipment

Get clients who come to you with the design already done.

Brigaplan is looking for partner installers, one or more per area. Clients design their kitchen or bar with us; when they are ready to buy, they come to you with the floor plan, the equipment list and the connections ready.

How it works for you

  1. 1The client gets the previewThey upload the floor plan or describe the space, and get a free floor plan, 3D model and a cost estimate for the equipment only.
  2. 2They go ahead with the designThey pay for it. The AI prepares it with the engine; if there are errors a designer fixes them, and if the client wants, a designer also checks it.
  3. 3They come to youYou get the client in your area with the design, the equipment list and the connections.
  4. 4You make the real quoteYou sell and install. If they buy from you, the cost of the design is deducted from their order.

What you gain

  • Clients who already know what they want and how much space they have, instead of wasted site visits.
  • No more hours of design work given away to people who then do not buy.
  • Quotes based on a precise design, with dimensions from the manufacturers' data sheets.
  • With the construction drawings, elevations and tables of the electrical, water, gas and drain connections, in PDF, DXF and CSV, to take on site after checking the dimensions on site.

What we ask of you

  • An office and a team in the area you cover.
  • Sales, installation and servicing of commercial kitchen equipment.
  • Real quotes, quickly, on the designs you receive.
  • Respect for the design: changes are agreed with the designer.

Who is behind it

Brigaplan is a service of Italfrigo Service S.r.l., which is also the first partner installer in Lombardy. The site always states which installers it works with.

Areas, exclusivity and fees are agreed together, partner by partner.
