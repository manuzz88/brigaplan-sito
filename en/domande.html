What is free and what you pay for

How can I try it?

For now Brigaplan is in a trial phase and you can only join by invitation: Italfrigo gives you the code, together with the link to sign up. If you would like to try it, write to info@brigaplan.ai.

What is free?

The preview: floor plan with zones and counters without dimensions, simplified 3D, generic equipment and a cost estimate range. AI prepares it and a designer checks it. Each account has a limited number of free previews.

What do I pay for?

The design, with a dimensioned floor plan, brands and models, full 3D and renders, and the construction drawings, with the drawings for the job site and the connections. The design costs €290 up to 60 covers or for a bar, €490 up to 150 covers and from €890 above that, with 3 rounds of changes included; the construction drawings cost €690, €1,190 or are quoted on request. Prices exclude VAT; including VAT: €353.80, €597.80 and from €1,085.80 for the design, €841.80 and €1,451.80 for the construction drawings. Before work starts you receive an offer with price and timing: you only pay if you accept it.

If I buy the equipment, do I still pay for the design?

If you buy from a partner installer, the cost of the design is deducted from your order and the construction drawings are free.

Do I have to buy from a partner?

No. If you buy from a partner, the cost of the design is deducted from your order and the construction drawings are free.

Installation and areas

Who installs the kitchen?

A partner installer in your area. The first is Italfrigo, in Lombardy. We are looking for partners in other regions.

Which areas do you cover?

At first we handle projects in Lombardy, where our partner is Italfrigo. We are looking for installers in other regions: if you are outside Lombardy, write to us and we will tell you when there is a partner near you.

Do the rules you check apply everywhere?

The engine checks the rules of the European Union and Italy, including those of Lombardy and Milan. Elsewhere, local rules must be checked with a local professional. For permits (for example from the local health authority and the planning office) you usually need a licensed professional anyway.

The preview

Do I need a floor plan of my venue?

No. If you don't have one, we ask you a few questions: dimensions, doors, windows and where the dining room is. A photo or a sketch also works, as long as it shows at least one known measurement.

Which files can I upload?

DXF, DWG, PDF or photos, up to 5 files of 25 MB each.

How long does it take?

The assistant prepares the preview in a few minutes. Then a designer checks it: we email you when it is ready.

Is the preview enough to build the kitchen?

No. It helps you understand space, equipment footprint and costs. To build, you need the design and the construction drawings, signed by a designer. In Italy, the paperwork for the ASL (health authority) and the SUAP (business permits office) usually also needs a licensed professional.

Why does the preview have no dimensions or brands?

Because it's free and it shows you the result: AI prepares it and a designer checks it. Dimensions, brands and models come with the paid design.

Your data and the floor plan

What happens to the floor plans I upload?

They stay confidential: only you and the designer checking your project can see them. We use them only for your project and share them with a partner only if you ask us to. When you close your account we delete them within 30 days.

Does the AI use my projects to learn?

No. We do not use them to train models, and the terms of our AI provider forbid it too.

Who sees my data?

You and authorised Brigaplan staff. Our technical providers (servers in Germany and the AI service) process them only on our behalf. You will find everything in the platform privacy notice.

Can I delete my account?

Yes, whenever you want, from the dashboard or by writing to info@brigaplan.ai. We delete the account, projects and files within 30 days.
