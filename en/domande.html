What is free and what you pay for

How can I try it?

You sign up for free in a minute and upload the floor plan or describe the venue. For any question, write to info@brigaplan.ai.

What is free?

The preview: floor plan with zones and counters without dimensions, simplified 3D, generic equipment and an equipment estimate in three ranges (budget, mid-range, premium) at list prices, in total and by zone, excluding transport, installation, connections, building services and VAT. In the preview the floor plan is read through the magnifier and the 3D has a watermark; in the full design everything is sharp, with dimensions and the 3D free to explore. The AI prepares it and the engine checks it against the rules. With each account you try the first preview for free, always complete (with a verified email), and one change. Then you continue with credits: 10 credits €10, 30 credits €25, VAT included. Over 500 m² of work areas the preview is not automatic: you can work on one zone at a time, each within 500 m², or go to a dealer or an architect.

What do I pay for?

The full design, with a furnished floor plan, a legend with type, dimensions, power supply and power rating (brand and code for manufacturers who have joined), full 3D you can rotate online and overall views, in PDF; and the construction drawings, which add elevations and connection tables, a services plan and DXF and CSV files. Launch prices, based on the m² of the work areas: design from €35 including VAT (€29 + VAT), construction drawings from €121 including VAT (€99 + VAT). The engine prepares them: you can buy them when the version has no blocking errors, and you receive them right after online payment. You see the exact price on your preview before buying. All prices including VAT, with examples, are on the prices page.

Are there subscriptions for professionals?

Yes, for dealers and installers with a VAT number: €39 + VAT a month with 60 credits included (one area) or €79 + VAT with 150 credits (several areas), from “My account” › “Your plan”. For architects and designers with a VAT number: €39 + VAT a month with 60 credits included. With credits: a new project costs 3 credits and a change 1 up to 150 m² of work areas; over 150 and up to 500 m², 6 credits and 2. For manufacturers the basic membership is free.

Do I have to buy from a dealer?

No. The design is yours and you can use it with any supplier you prefer. If you want, from the project page you ask for a quote from no more than 3 verified dealers or installers, or you entrust it to a single one from the list with "Entrust the project": you are not obliged to buy from them.

Installation and areas

Who installs the kitchen?

The dealer or installer you choose. For equipment and installation, ask the dealers and installers in your area for a quote, from the project page.

Where can Brigaplan be used?

You get the preview, the design and the construction drawings online. For equipment and installation, ask the dealers and installers in your area for a quote; otherwise you use the design with the supplier you prefer.

Do the rules you check apply everywhere?

The engine checks the rules of the European Union and Italy, including those of Lombardy and Milan. Elsewhere, local rules must be checked with a local professional. For permits (for example from the local health authority and the planning office) you usually need a licensed professional anyway.

The preview

Do I need a floor plan of my venue?

No. If you don't have one, we ask you a few questions: dimensions, doors, windows and where the dining room is. A photo or a sketch also works, as long as it shows at least one known measurement.

What is it worth knowing before you start?

The floor plan, if you have one. Then, if you know them: the energy used for cooking (gas, electric or both), the electrical power available in kW, which you find on your electricity bill or meter, and whether there is already a gas connection, with its capacity. If you don't know them yet, you can still get the preview.

Which files can I upload?

DXF, DWG, PDF or photos, up to 5 files of 10 MB each.

How long does it take?

The assistant prepares the preview in a few minutes and you see it straight away, as an automatic draft. If the engine finds problems it points them out: the assistant fixes them free of charge and, if it cannot, tells you what still needs fixing: you can ask a dealer or your technician. You can buy the design and the construction drawings when the version has no blocking errors, and you receive them right after online payment.

Is the preview enough to build the kitchen?

No. It helps you understand spaces, footprints and costs. Brigaplan gives you a functional equipment layout. For health authority (ASL), HACCP, fire safety (Vigili del fuoco) and building permits you need a design signed by a qualified professional of your choice, and the layout must be approved by your consultants. Before work starts on site, the installer checks dimensions and connections.

Why does the preview have no dimensions or brands?

Because it is free and helps you understand the result: the AI prepares it and the engine checks it against the rules. In the full design, brand and code appear only for manufacturers who have joined Brigaplan; for other equipment we give type, dimensions, power supply and power rating. The dimensioned connections come with the construction drawings.

Your data and the floor plan

What happens to the floor plans I upload?

They stay confidential: they are seen by you and, to prepare the preview, by the AI provider, which works only on our behalf. Someone from our staff opens them only if you ask for our help, and for quality they may look at a sample of previews and the floor plan as you uploaded it, without your profile data, unless you object. We share them with a dealer or an installer only if you ask us to, and we never show them to other users. Projects, without names or contact details, help us improve Brigaplan; you can object at any time from your account. When you close your account we delete them straight away; in backup copies they remain for 14 days at most.

Does the AI use my projects to learn?

We do not use them to train artificial intelligence models, and the AI provider may not do so. We use them, without names or contact details, to improve Brigaplan's rules and internal examples; you can object at any time from your account.

Who sees my data?

You and, only if you ask for our help, authorised Brigaplan staff. To check quality, staff may look at a sample of previews and the floor plan as you uploaded it, without your profile data, unless you object. Technical providers (servers in Germany, AI service, email delivery) process them only on our behalf. You will find everything in the platform privacy notice.

Can I delete my account?

Yes, whenever you want, from the dashboard or by writing to info@brigaplan.ai. We delete your account, projects and files straight away; in backup copies they remain for 14 days at most. For paid projects we keep a closed copy for 10 years, and invoices for as long as the law requires.
