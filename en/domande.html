What is free and what you pay for

How can I try it?

For now Brigaplan is in a trial phase and you can only join by invitation: Italfrigo gives you the code, together with the link to sign up. If you would like to try it, write to info@brigaplan.ai.

What is free?

The preview: floor plan with zones and counters without dimensions, simplified 3D, generic equipment and a cost estimate range. AI prepares it and a designer checks it. Each account can create 2 projects with a free preview, each with up to 3 versions.

What do I pay for?

The design, with a dimensioned floor plan, brands and models, full 3D to rotate online, and the construction drawings, with the drawings to take to the job site and the connections. Including VAT, the design costs €353.80 up to 60 covers or for a bar, €597.80 up to 150 covers and from €1,085.80 above that, with 3 rounds of changes included; the construction drawings cost €841.80, €1,451.80 or are quoted on request. Excluding VAT, that is €290, €490 and from €890 for the design, €690 and €1,190 for the construction drawings. Before work starts you receive an offer with price and timing: you only pay if you accept it.

If I buy the equipment, do I still pay for the design?

If you buy from a partner installer, the cost of the design is deducted from your order and the construction drawings are included in the order.

Do I have to buy from a partner?

No. The design is yours and you can use it with any supplier you prefer. If you buy from a partner installer, the cost of the design is deducted from your order and the construction drawings are included in the order.

Installation and areas

Who installs the kitchen?

A partner installer in your area. In Lombardy it is Italfrigo Service S.r.l., the same company that runs Brigaplan. We are looking for partners in other regions.

Which areas do you cover?

At first we handle projects in Lombardy, where our partner is Italfrigo. We are looking for installers in other regions: if you are outside Lombardy, write to us and we will tell you when there is a partner near you.

Do the rules you check apply everywhere?

The engine checks the rules of the European Union and Italy, including those of Lombardy and Milan. Elsewhere, local rules must be checked with a local professional. For permits (for example from the local health authority and the planning office) you usually need a licensed professional anyway.

The preview

Do I need a floor plan of my venue?

No. If you don't have one, we ask you a few questions: dimensions, doors, windows and where the dining room is. A photo or a sketch also works, as long as it shows at least one known measurement.

Which files can I upload?

DXF, DWG, PDF or photos, up to 5 files of 10 MB each.

How long does it take?

The assistant prepares the preview in a few minutes. If the engine finds no errors you see it right away, as an automatic draft; then a designer checks it and we email you. If something needs fixing, you see it when it is ready.

Is the preview enough to build the kitchen?

No. It helps you understand space, equipment footprint and costs. To build, you need the design and the construction drawings, overseen by a designer from our staff. In Italy, the paperwork for the ASL (health authority) and the SUAP (business permits office), and the design of the building services where the law requires it, also need a licensed professional.

Why does the preview have no dimensions or brands?

Because it's free and it shows you the result: AI prepares it and a designer checks it. Dimensions, brands and models come with the paid design.

Your data and the floor plan

What happens to the floor plans I upload?

They stay confidential: they are seen by you, the staff working on your project and, to prepare the preview, the AI provider, which works only on our behalf. We share them with a partner only if you ask us to, and we never show them to other users. Projects, without names or contact details, help us improve Brigaplan; you can object at any time from your account. When you close the account we delete them immediately; in the backups they remain for 14 days at most.

Does the AI use my projects to learn?

We do not use them to train artificial intelligence models, and the AI provider may not do so. We use them, without names or contact details, to improve Brigaplan's rules and internal examples; you can object at any time from your account.

Who sees my data?

You and authorised Brigaplan staff. Our technical providers (servers in Germany, the AI service, email delivery) process them only on our behalf. You will find everything in the platform privacy notice.

Can I delete my account?

Yes, whenever you want, from the dashboard or by writing to info@brigaplan.ai. We delete the account, projects and files immediately; in the backups they remain for 14 days at most.
