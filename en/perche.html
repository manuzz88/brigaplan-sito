Why Brigaplan

Your commercial kitchen, designed without drawing a line.

You don't need to know how to use drawing software. Upload the floor plan or describe your space, answer the questions of the trade and in a few minutes you see the kitchen: floor plan, 3D and cost estimate. Then, if you want, the full design and the construction drawings, made by the same engine.

  • Start from what you have.

    A floor plan in DXF, DWG or PDF, or a photo of the floor plan with at least one measurement written on it. If you don't have a floor plan, describe the space: dimensions, doors, windows. We ask you to confirm the dimensions read from PDFs and photos.

  • The assistant asks the questions of the trade.

    Before drawing, it asks for what it needs: energy supply and available power, hood and flue, menu, doors, refrigeration, budget range. If you don't know something, it starts from reasonable values and marks them for you to confirm.

  • A rules engine checks every version.

    About a hundred types of professional check, around 50 on each version depending on the type of kitchen. If the engine finds an error, the assistant corrects it before showing you the preview.

  • Free preview in a few minutes.

    Floor plan with zones and counters, 3D you can rotate in your browser, cost estimate for the equipment in three ranges: budget, mid-range and premium.

  • Real equipment from the manufacturers.

    Over 23,000 pieces of equipment from 80 brands in the catalogue, with dimensions confirmed by the manufacturers' data sheets and drawings. In the full design and the construction drawings we use only equipment with dimensions from official manufacturer data sheets, drawings or price lists, with brands and product codes in the legend.

  • If a piece of equipment is missing, we add it.

    If you are a professional, you upload the data sheet in PDF: we read the dimensions and connections, you confirm the values and the machine is in your projects straight away.

  • Construction drawings straight from the engine, with no hand drafting.

    PDF drawing sheets (floor plan, elevations, sections and legend), connection tables, drawings for the electrician, plumber, refrigeration technician and extraction installer, DXF and CSV files, a package for the site.

  • Clear prices, per project.

    The preview is free. Full design from €35 and construction drawings from €121, including VAT, based on the square metres of the work areas. No compulsory subscription; for professionals there are optional subscriptions from €39 + VAT a month.

  • For professionals, your way of working.

    Your studio rules stay saved and apply to your later projects. With a subscription, your brand goes on the full design and the construction drawings.

  • A real person, if you want one.

    A check by a designer is optional and paid. Installation by a partner installer, currently in Lombardy (Italy).

  • Inside Claude too.

    With the Brigaplan connector you can also prepare the preview by talking to Claude, Anthropic's assistant.

Brigaplan gives you a functional equipment layout. For health authority (ASL), HACCP, fire safety (Vigili del fuoco) and building permits you need a design signed by a qualified professional of your choice, and that professional checks that the premises comply.
