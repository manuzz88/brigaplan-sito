Why Brigaplan

Your commercial kitchen, designed without drawing a line.

You don't need to know how to use drawing software. Upload the floor plan or describe your space, answer the questions of the trade and in a few minutes you see the kitchen: floor plan, 3D and an estimate in three price ranges. Then, if you want, the full design and the construction drawings, made by the same engine.

  • Start from what you have.

    A floor plan in DXF, DWG or PDF, or a photo of the floor plan with at least one measurement written on it. If you don't have a floor plan, describe the space: dimensions, doors, windows. We ask you to confirm the dimensions read from PDFs and photos.

  • The assistant asks the questions of the trade.

    Before drawing, it asks for what it needs: energy supply and available power, hood and flue, menu, doors, refrigeration. If you don't know something, it starts from reasonable values and marks them for you to confirm.

  • A rules engine checks every version.

    About a hundred types of professional check, around 50 on each version depending on the type of kitchen. If the engine finds an error, the assistant corrects it before showing you the preview.

  • Free preview in a few minutes.

    Floor plan with zones and counters, 3D you can rotate in your browser, equipment estimate in three ranges (budget, mid-range and premium) at list prices, in total and by zone.

  • Real equipment from the manufacturers.

    Over 8,800 pieces of equipment from 64 brands in the catalogue, with dimensions confirmed by the manufacturers' data sheets, catalogues and drawings. In the full design and the construction drawings we use equipment with dimensions taken from manufacturers' data sheets, drawings or price lists; if the dimensions of a piece of equipment are not yet confirmed, we mark it “to be checked”: check them against the data sheet before ordering. Brand and code appear only for manufacturers who have joined Brigaplan; for other equipment we give type, dimensions, power supply and power rating.

  • If a piece of equipment is missing, we add it.

    If you are a professional, you upload the data sheet in PDF: we read the dimensions and connections, you confirm the values and the machine is in your projects straight away.

  • Construction drawings straight from the engine, with no hand drafting.

    PDF drawing sheets (floor plan, elevations, sections and legend), connection tables, drawings for the electrician, plumber, refrigeration technician and extraction installer, DXF and CSV files, a package for the site.

  • Clear prices, per project.

    The preview is free. Full design from €35 and construction drawings from €121, including VAT, based on the square metres of the work areas. No compulsory subscription to use Brigaplan; for dealers and installers the subscription, from €39 + VAT a month, is needed to receive clients' requests. With a subscription and verification, you pay for the drawings in credits with the all-in design, from 10 credits.

  • For professionals, your way of working.

    Your studio rules stay saved and apply to your later projects. With a subscription, your brand goes on the full design and the construction drawings.

  • The checks are done by whoever you choose.

    You can have the design checked by a dealer in your area, or download the files and take them to your technician. For equipment and installation, ask the dealers and installers in your area for a quote.

  • Inside Claude too.

    With the Brigaplan connector you can also prepare the preview by talking to Claude, Anthropic's assistant.

Brigaplan gives you a functional equipment layout. For health authority (ASL), HACCP, fire safety (Vigili del fuoco) and building permits you need a design signed by a qualified professional of your choice, and that professional checks that the premises comply.
