Commercial kitchen design software: a guide for dealers

A foodservice equipment dealer spends many hours drawing kitchens and bars for clients who often do not buy. Here you will find how to use Brigaplan to make the first proposal in a few minutes, what you can already do today and what comes with the manufacturers' brands.

What you do today

  • The first proposal: you upload your client's floor plan (DXF, DWG, PDF or a photo) or describe the venue. The AI prepares the kitchen or bar and the engine checks it against the rules of the trade. You refine it with the assistant.
  • Your logo: with your brand active, the full design and the construction drawings carry your logo and your letterhead. The free preview stays with the Brigaplan brand.
  • Your price lists: you upload them with your discounts. Only you can see them, and they are encrypted.
  • Your machines: if a piece of equipment is missing, you upload it with its technical data sheet. If it passes the checks, it goes into the projects.
  • Your studio rules: when you correct a project, the assistant asks you whether it should remember it. Your choices apply only to your account.
  • The construction drawings: elevations, connection tables, services plan, and DXF and CSV files for your own drawings.

Brands in the projects

In Brigaplan projects, the equipment has no brand. The legend gives the type, dimensions, power supply and power rating of each piece; brand and code only for manufacturers who have joined Brigaplan. In the free preview there are no brands or codes.

  • Why: a brand appears only with the consent of its manufacturer. No brand is named today.
  • The dimensions: they come from the manufacturers' data sheets, catalogues and drawings. If a dimension is not confirmed yet, the project marks it "to be checked".
  • The model: you choose it, with the brands you sell.

Access granted by manufacturers

A manufacturer that has joined can give you access to its brands. The manufacturer decides.

  • What it can grant you: its product library, technical data sheets and the price list for price ranges; if it chooses, the list prices too.
  • By area: library, data sheets and price ranges also to all verified dealers based in an area. What counts is the head office resulting from the verification, not the areas you serve. List prices only to one dealer at a time.
  • Ask for access to a brand: once your company is verified, you ask the manufacturer for access from your account, and the manufacturer approves it or not. You can only ask for a brand that its manufacturer has switched on in Brigaplan.
  • What the manufacturer sees: if you ask it for a brand or choose to let manufacturers in your area find you, your company name, the areas you serve and the status of the access it has granted you. Never the other brands you sell, your clients or your projects.

Coming soon: projects with your brands

These features are not online yet. We will announce them on the site's pages when they start.

  • Invite the manufacturer: if you sell a brand that has not joined yet, you send the manufacturer a ready-made invitation yourself. Until it joins, that brand stays unnamed in the projects.
  • Projects with your brands: the projects you make use the brands you have been granted. Where those brands do not have the equipment that is needed, the engine chooses another piece, which stays without a brand.
  • Your link: a client who comes in through one of your links starts with your brands already in place.

Requests from restaurant owners in your area

After the preview, a restaurant owner can send their project to several dealers to get a quote.

  • How you receive them: with a professional account with a VAT number, you switch on "I receive requests" and enter the areas you serve. First we check that your business is in the sector.
  • How many: each request goes to at most 3 dealers, chosen by the restaurant owner. You have 14 days to reply.
  • Order: on the request page the client sees the dealers in their area first, then those in their country, then the others. Within each group the order is random, changes every day and is the same for everyone: nobody pays to be higher up.
  • Areas: no area is exclusive.
  • The client's data: you use it only for that quote and, if the client accepts it, to contact them.
  • Cost: today, receiving and answering requests is free.

The public list of dealers

Anyone looking for a dealer or an installer chooses the area and, if they want, the brand.

  • How you get in: once your company is verified, you tick the box in your profile. You can untick it whenever you want, and your listing disappears from Brigaplan straight away.
  • What your listing shows: company name, town and country of the head office, areas served, description, languages, website, number of technicians and year founded. Never the address, email, phone numbers or names of people.
  • Order: random, changes every day and is the same for everyone; visitors can also sort by name. Nobody pays to be higher up.
  • Brands: a brand appears next to your name only if the manufacturer has granted it to you and chosen to show it, and you have chosen to show it on your listing.
  • "Verified": it means we have checked that the business exists and that the people who run it belong to it. It is not a judgement on the quality of the work.
  • Search engines: listings are not indexed.

How much it costs

  • To start: the first preview and one change are free, then you continue with credits.
  • Full design and construction drawings: you pay per project, online by card. The prices are on the prices page.
  • Subscriptions for dealers and installers: coming soon, not on sale yet. If you want to be among the first, write to us.
  • Commissions: Brigaplan does not receive commissions on sales and does not pay any to anyone. The supply and installation contract is between you and your client.

What Brigaplan does and does not do

  • It does: the functional equipment layout, checked by the engine, with the connection diagram in the construction drawings.
  • It does not do: the paperwork and the compliance of the venue. These need the design and the signature of a licensed technician chosen by the client. Before work starts on site, the installer checks dimensions and connections.
