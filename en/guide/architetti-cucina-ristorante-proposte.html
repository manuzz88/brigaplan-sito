Restaurant kitchen for architects: design and proposals

An architect or a consultant working on a restaurant has to fit the kitchen into the project and then help the client choose who supplies and installs it. Here you will find how to use Brigaplan for the kitchen, what you can do and how to ask dealers for several proposals.

What you do today

  • The kitchen inside your project: you upload the floor plan (DXF, DWG, PDF or a photo) or describe the venue. The AI lays out zones, counters and flows, and the engine checks them against the rules of the trade. You decide and refine with the assistant.
  • The construction drawings: elevations, connection tables, services plan and the DXF to put in your own drawings.
  • Your studio: with your brand active, the full design and the construction drawings carry your studio's logo and letterhead. The brand is included in the subscription for architects and designers, which you switch on in "My account" › "Your plan".
  • Your studio rules: when you correct a project, the assistant asks you whether it should remember it. Your choices apply only to your account.
  • Without brands: the legend gives the type, dimensions, power supply and power rating of each piece of equipment; brand and code only for manufacturers who have joined. The client chooses the model, also together with their dealer.

Up to 3 proposals to compare

From the project page you send the version you see to no more than 3 verified dealers or installers, or from the public list you entrust the project to a single one, with "Entrust the project". Asking for a quote is always free.

  • Who you send it to: each dealer you choose receives the same project. The page shows you only the dealers that can answer.
  • The client's consent: by ticking the box you confirm that your client asked you to send it to those dealers for an offer and knows that, from that moment, each of them uses this data for the offer: you are responsible for this. The client's contact details are sent only if you tick the second box; your contact details are always sent.
  • What the dealer receives: the type of venue, the number of covers, the floor plan and the 3D of the preview, the version number, the description and your note without email addresses, links or long numbers, and your contact details (name, company, email). With the second box, also the city, and the full description and note. Never the project name or the conversation with the assistant. Before sending, you see exactly what they receive.
  • Who replies: no dealer is obliged to reply. Those who reply send you their proposal with the amount, excluding VAT, and if they wish a note and their quote as a PDF: you find it on the project page.
  • The comparison: you see the proposals side by side. Each dealer knows only how many dealers the request was sent to, not who they are, and does not see the other dealers' proposals.
  • The deadline: each dealer has 14 days to reply. If you withdraw the request, dealers that have not yet sent you a proposal can no longer see it.

Transparency towards the client

Brigaplan does not provide for or handle fees between architects and dealers. If you receive a fee or a benefit from a dealer or a manufacturer for a project assigned through Brigaplan, it is your own arrangement: you must follow the rules of your profession and of your country, which in some cases forbid it, and in any case tell your client.

How much it costs

  • To start: the first preview and one change are free, then you continue with credits.
  • Full design and construction drawings: you pay per project, online by card or, where your phone allows it, with Apple Pay or Google Pay. The prices are on the prices page.
  • Subscription for architects and designers: €39 + VAT a month with 60 credits included every month. You activate it by card from "My account", in the "Your plan" section.

What Brigaplan does and does not do

  • It does: the functional equipment layout, checked by the engine.
  • It does not do: the compliance of the venue and the paperwork. Heights, floor areas, toilets, changing rooms and permits are checked by the licensed technician who follows the project. Before work starts on site, the installer checks dimensions and connections.
