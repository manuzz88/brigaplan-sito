Foodservice equipment manufacturers: your brand in designs

Brigaplan designs commercial kitchens and bars with the dimensions of real equipment. In the designs, the equipment has no brand: a brand appears only if its manufacturer joins and gives consent. Here you will find how to join, what the manufacturer decides and what comes next.

Why brands are anonymous

  • Consent first: no brand appears in the designs without the manufacturer's consent.
  • What the client sees: in the legend, the type, dimensions, power supply and power rating of each piece of equipment. Brand and code only in paid projects (full design, construction drawings, subscribers' projects) and only for manufacturers who have given consent; no brands or codes in the free preview.
  • Who comes first: the first manufacturers to join are also the first that clients see in the designs.

How to join

  • Sign-up: you sign up as a manufacturer, or someone acting for you does. The basic membership is free.
  • Your brands: you list the brands you make.
  • Your library: technical data sheets, dimensions, connections and CAD drawings.
  • The manufacturer dashboard: already in your account, for permissions.
  • Permissions: separate from each other. For example, using the technical data of your products in clients' designs, and showing your brands. You give and withdraw them whenever you want from your account.
  • Your team: you invite colleagues into your company with a link that is valid for 60 days and can be used once, each with their own role: owner, person who manages brand access, or collaborator.
  • The check: we check the data before it goes into clients' designs.

What changes in the designs

  • More models: the models you give us go into the designs, not only those from public data sheets.
  • Data as you provide it: dimensions, connections and drawings, updated when you send us new ones.
  • Connections from your drawings: in the construction drawings, the position and power of the connections come from your dimensioned drawings, not from typical positions.

Your files and your price list

  • The price list is optional: if you upload it, we store it encrypted. We do not show it to clients or to other manufacturers.
  • Where the files are kept: all the details are on the Manufacturers page.

Access for dealers

With your company verified and consent given for the brand, you decide who uses your data.

  • For each dealer: to a dealer who asks you for a brand, you grant your library, technical data sheets and the price list for price ranges and, if you want and the dealer is verified, list prices, with separate permissions. You can also offer it yourself to a dealer who lets manufacturers in their area find them: the dealer accepts or not.
  • By area: you give library, data sheets and price ranges to all verified dealers based in a country, a region or a province. What counts is the head office resulting from the verification, not the areas they serve. You do not see who they are. List prices stay for individual verified dealers only.
  • Requests: a dealer with a company on Brigaplan can ask you for access to a brand you have switched on. You see whether their company is verified or not, and you decide.
  • Whenever you want: you change or withdraw access whenever you want. Every change is recorded.
  • What you see: of the dealers who ask you for a brand or choose to let manufacturers in their area find them, the company name, whether it is verified, their areas and the access they have from you. Never the other brands they sell, their clients or their designs.
  • In the public list: you can choose to show your brand next to the dealers you have granted it to, in the public list of dealers. It appears only if the dealer also chooses to show it.

What Brigaplan does not do

  • Commissions: Brigaplan does not receive commissions on sales and does not pay any to anyone.
  • Sales: Brigaplan does not sell equipment and does not install it. The client buys from a dealer or an installer of their own choice.
