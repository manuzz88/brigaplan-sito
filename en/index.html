FAQ

In short.

Is it really free?

Yes. With your account you create 2 projects with the free preview, each with up to 3 versions. You only pay if you want the full design.

Do I have to have the floor plan?

No. We ask you a few questions: dimensions, doors, windows. A photo or a sketch with at least one measurement is fine too.

Is the preview enough to build?

No: it helps you understand space, footprint and costs. For ASL, HACCP, the fire brigade and building permits you need the design and the signature of a qualified technician of your choice.

Who sees my floor plans?

You. Our staff open them only if you ask for a check, the full design or our help, or for a sample quality check, which you can object to. Everything is in the platform privacy notice.
