Today a kitchen design gets done, redone and often given away.

Anyone opening or renovating a restaurant, a bar or a hotel almost always goes through the same loop.

  1. A design from each supplier

    Each one sends a technician, takes measurements and draws its own kitchen.

  2. Every change, another round

    The design is redone every time the client changes their mind. Weeks go by.

  3. Quotes you can't compare

    Different machines, different sizes, different prices. The client can't tell who is right.

  4. And often, no sale

    Or the client takes the design to another supplier to get a lower price.

The supplier worked for free, the client lost weeks, and the loop starts again with someone else.

Who loses

The client

Waits, compares proposals that don't match, and decides without a design of their own.

The supplier

Gives away hours of design work. Sometimes that work ends up in a competitor's hands.

Architects and designers

They chase data sheets, dimensions and CAD blocks for every brand, and wait for the supplier at every revision.

In the end, the cost of all those lost designs ends up in the prices, and everyone pays for it.

The design is done once, properly, before you buy.

With Brigaplan you pay for the design separately from the sale: designers get paid to design, and buyers go to the supplier knowing what they want.

Manufacturers' dimensions

Over 27,000 pieces of equipment from more than 80 brands, with dimensions taken from the manufacturers' catalogues and data sheets. Never from memory, never made up.

Real kitchen rules

The engine checks every version against the rules of the trade, more than 40 of them measured automatically: separate routes for clean and dirty, wide enough passages, hoods to the EN 16282 standard. If it finds errors it points them out, and the assistant fixes them free of charge.

Who sees your floor plans

No one on our staff opens your projects unless you ask for a check, the full design or our help: they are processed by the engine and the AI. To check quality, we may look at a sample of previews and the floor plan as you uploaded it, without your profile data; if you object from your account, no one looks at yours.

Who sees them

You. Someone from our staff only if you ask for a check, the full design or our help, and every opening is logged. For quality, they may look at a sample of previews and the floor plan as you uploaded it, without your profile data, unless you object. We never show them to other users and we do not give them to a partner unless you ask us to.

Where they are kept

On the platform's servers, in Germany. To prepare the preview the AI provider also receives them; it also processes them in the United States under the standard contractual clauses, works only on our behalf and may not use them to train its models.

How long they stay

As long as you keep your account. When you close it we delete them straight away; in backup copies they remain for 14 days at most.

How to delete them

Close your account from the dashboard whenever you want, or write to info@brigaplan.ai. From your account you can also object to the sample check of previews and to the use of projects to improve the service.

All the details are in the platform privacy notice.

The questions we get most often.

Do I need a floor plan of my venue?

No. If you don't have one, we ask you a few questions: dimensions, doors, windows and where the dining room is. A photo or a sketch also works, as long as it shows at least one known measurement.

Is the preview enough to build the kitchen?

No. It helps you understand spaces, footprints and costs. To build, you need the design and the construction drawings, which a designer from our staff follows. Brigaplan gives you a functional equipment layout. For health authority (ASL), HACCP, fire safety (Vigili del fuoco) and building permits you need a design signed by a qualified professional of your choice, and the layout must be approved by your consultants. Before work starts on site, the installer checks dimensions and connections.

Why does the preview have no dimensions or brands?

Because it is free and helps you understand the result: the AI prepares it and the engine checks it against the rules. Dimensions, brands and models come with the paid design.

Who installs the kitchen?

A partner installer in your area. In Lombardy it is Italfrigo Service S.r.l., the company that runs Brigaplan. We are looking for partners in other areas.

Do I have to buy from a partner?

No. The design is yours and you can use it with any supplier you prefer. If you buy from a partner installer, the cost of the design is deducted from your order.

What happens to the floor plans I upload?

They stay confidential: they are seen by you and, to prepare the preview, by the AI provider, which works only on our behalf. Someone from our staff opens them only if you ask for a check, the full design or our help, and for quality they may look at a sample of previews and the floor plan as you uploaded it, without your profile data, unless you object. We share them with a partner only if you ask us to, and we never show them to other users. Projects, without names or contact details, help us improve Brigaplan; you can object at any time from your account.
