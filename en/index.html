Today a kitchen design gets done, redone and often given away.

Anyone opening or renovating a restaurant, a bar or a hotel almost always goes through the same loop.

  1. A design from each supplier

    Each one sends a technician, takes measurements and draws its own kitchen.

  2. Every change, another round

    The design is redone every time the client changes their mind. Weeks go by.

  3. Quotes you can't compare

    Different machines, different sizes, different prices. The client can't tell who is right.

  4. And often, no sale

    Or the client takes the design to another supplier to get a lower price.

The supplier worked for free, the client lost weeks, and the loop starts again with someone else.

Who loses

The client

Waits, compares proposals that don't match, and decides without a design of their own.

The supplier

Gives away hours of design work. Sometimes that work ends up in a competitor's hands.

Architects and designers

They chase data sheets, dimensions and CAD blocks for every brand, and wait for the supplier at every revision.

In the end, the cost of all those lost designs ends up in the prices, and everyone pays for it.

The questions we get most often.

Do I need a floor plan of my venue?

No. If you don't have one, we ask you a few questions: dimensions, doors, windows and where the dining room is. A photo or a sketch also works, as long as it shows at least one known measurement.

Is the preview enough to build the kitchen?

No. It helps you understand space, equipment footprint and costs. To build, you need the design and the construction drawings, overseen by a designer from our staff. In Italy, the paperwork for the ASL (health authority) and the SUAP (business permits office), and the design of the building services where the law requires it, also need a licensed professional.

Why does the preview have no dimensions or brands?

Because it's free and it shows you the result: AI prepares it and a designer checks it. Dimensions, brands and models come with the paid design.

Who installs the kitchen?

A partner installer in your area. In Lombardy it is Italfrigo Service S.r.l., the same company that runs Brigaplan. We are looking for partners in other regions.

Do I have to buy from a partner?

No. The design is yours and you can use it with any supplier you prefer. If you buy from a partner installer, the cost of the design is deducted from your order.

What happens to the floor plans I upload?

They stay confidential: they are seen by you, the staff working on your project and, to prepare the preview, the AI provider, which works only on our behalf. We share them with a partner only if you ask us to, and we never show them to other users. Projects, without names or contact details, help us improve Brigaplan; you can object at any time from your account.
