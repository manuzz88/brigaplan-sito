What a preview is, and what it is not

  • The preview is indicative: it helps you understand space, footprint and costs. It is not a set of construction drawings and it is not enough to build.
  • The preview is prepared by an artificial intelligence assistant and our design engine: a person sees it only if you ask for our help, or for the sample quality check, unless you object (never for professional accounts). The all-in design is prepared by the engine.
  • Brigaplan gives you a functional equipment layout. For health authority (ASL), HACCP, fire safety (Vigili del fuoco) and building permits you need a design signed by a qualified professional of your choice, and the layout must be approved by your consultants. Before work starts on site, the installer checks dimensions and connections. Brigaplan does not sign code-compliant designs. The all-in design does not replace the building services design: when the law requires it (Ministerial Decree 37/2008), it is drawn up and signed by a registered professional.
  • An automatic draft has not been seen by a designer: it may contain errors. If the engine finds problems it points them out, and the assistant fixes them free of charge.
  • The estimate is based on the manufacturers' full list prices and is indicative: transport, installation, connections, building services and VAT are excluded. It is not an offer: the real price is set by whoever sells and installs the equipment.
  • In the preview and in the all-in design, if the dimensions of a piece of equipment are not yet confirmed by a manufacturer's data sheet, drawing or price list, we mark it “to be checked”: check it against the data sheet before ordering. In the all-in design, brand and code appear only for manufacturers who have joined Brigaplan; never in the preview, the preview of the all-in design or the renders. For other equipment we give type, dimensions and technical data: you choose the model, also with your dealer. Dimensions must always be checked before ordering.
  • We work with the measurements of the venue that you give us (floor plan, photos, description or, when it is available, scan with the app), without a site visit. If you scan the venue with the app and confirm a wall by measuring it with a tape measure, you are the one declaring that measurement: we record its date and value and use it to check the scale of the scan. The design is based on the measurements you give us: errors that come from a wrong measurement you gave us are not our responsibility, just as for an uploaded floor plan or a written measurement. Errors that depend on us remain our responsibility. For this reason all measurements, of the venue and of the connections, must be checked on site before ordering the equipment and starting the works: we also write this on the drawings.
  • The rules the engine checks are those of the European Union and Italy, including those of Lombardy and Milan. Elsewhere, local rules must be checked with a local professional.
