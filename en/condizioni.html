What the platform offers

  • Free preview: you upload the floor plan or describe the space and get a floor plan with zones and counters, a simplified 3D model and a cost estimate range. It is prepared by an artificial intelligence assistant together with our design engine, and a designer checks it before you see it.
  • Design and construction drawings: these are paid. Before work starts you receive an offer with price, contents and timing, which you accept separately.
  • Partner installers: if you ask us to, we put you in touch with an installer in your area.

Free previews

Each account can create 2 projects with a free preview, each with up to 3 versions. We may change these numbers for new projects, with notice. On the “My projects” page you can see how many you have left. If a preview fails because of a technical error on our side, it does not count, and neither do versions created from instructions by our staff.

What a preview is, and what it is not

  • The preview is indicative: it helps you understand space, footprint and costs. It is not a set of construction drawings and it is not enough to build.
  • Previews and designs are prepared by an artificial intelligence assistant and our design engine, together with the work of a designer: we tell you so on the platform.
  • To build you need the design and the construction drawings, and for permits (for example from the local health authority and the planning office) usually a licensed professional too. The construction drawings do not replace the design of the building services: where the law requires it (Ministerial Decree 37/2008), that design is produced and signed by a professional registered with the relevant professional body.
  • The cost estimate is an approximate range, not an offer: the real price is set by whoever sells and installs the equipment.
  • Equipment dimensions come from the manufacturers' data sheets, but they must always be checked before ordering.
  • We work with the measurements of the venue that you give us (floor plan, photos, description), without a site visit. For this reason all measurements, of the venue and of the connections, must be checked on site before ordering the equipment and starting the works: we also write this on the drawings.
  • The rules the engine checks are those of the European Union and Italy, including those of Lombardy and Milan. Elsewhere, local rules must be checked with a local professional.

Partner installers and purchases
