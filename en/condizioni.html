What the platform offers

  • Preview: you upload the floor plan or describe the venue and get a floor plan with zones and counters, a simplified 3D and a cost estimate as a range. It is prepared by an artificial intelligence assistant together with our design engine, which checks it against the rules. You see it straight away, labelled “AI draft, checked by the rules engine”: it is automatic work, and a designer checks it only if you ask. The first one is free: see Previews and credits.
  • Full design and construction drawings: the engine prepares them, for a fee. If the version has no errors you receive them straight after payment; if it has some, a designer fixes them within the time stated in the offer. See Paid services.
  • Check by a designer: if you ask for it, someone from our staff checks the preview, the design or the construction drawings. It is always optional and paid.
  • Partner installers: if you ask us to, we put you in touch with an installer in your area.

The service has just started: some features may change, be suspended or arrive later.

What a preview is, and what it is not

  • The preview is indicative: it helps you understand space, footprint and costs. It is not a set of construction drawings and it is not enough to build.
  • The preview is prepared by an artificial intelligence assistant and our design engine: a person sees it only if you ask for a designer's check, for a fee, or for the sample quality check, unless you object (never for professional accounts). The full design and the construction drawings are prepared by the engine; if the version still has open problems, a designer fixes them.
  • Brigaplan gives you a functional equipment layout. For health authority (ASL), HACCP, fire safety (Vigili del fuoco) and building permits you need a design signed by a qualified professional of your choice, and the layout must be approved by your consultants. Before work starts on site, the installer checks dimensions and connections. Brigaplan does not sign code-compliant designs. The construction drawings do not replace the building services design: when the law requires it (Ministerial Decree 37/2008), it is drawn up and signed by a registered professional.
  • An automatic draft has not been seen by a designer: it may contain errors. If the engine finds problems it points them out, and the assistant fixes them free of charge.
  • The cost estimate is an approximate range, not an offer: the real price is set by whoever sells and installs the equipment.
  • Equipment dimensions come from the manufacturers' data sheets, but they must always be checked before ordering.
  • We work with the measurements of the venue that you give us (floor plan, photos, description), without a site visit. For this reason all measurements, of the venue and of the connections, must be checked on site before ordering the equipment and starting the works: we also write this on the drawings.
  • The rules the engine checks are those of the European Union and Italy, including those of Lombardy and Milan. Elsewhere, local rules must be checked with a local professional.
