What data we process today

The site does not use cookies, analytics or advertising tools, and does not load content from other sites: fonts and videos are on our own server.

For now the "Try it on your space" form does not send anything: what you type stays in your browser and never reaches us.

The only processing concerns the technical data of each visit, which the service hosting the site processes automatically to deliver the pages: IP address, date and time, page requested, browser type. We do not keep visit logs and we do not see the IP addresses of visitors.

Why, and on what legal basis

The technical data are needed to run the site and to protect it from abuse and attacks. The legal basis is the controller's legitimate interest in the security of the service (Article 6(1)(f) of the Regulation). We do not use them to profile you or for any other purpose.

Who processes them for us

The site is hosted by DigitalOcean LLC (App Platform service, Frankfurt region, Germany) and reaches visitors through the content delivery network (CDN) used by DigitalOcean, which also has nodes outside the European Union. DigitalOcean processes the technical data as a processor, on our behalf. It is a US company: transfers outside the European Union are covered by the safeguards provided for by the Regulation, such as standard contractual clauses.

For how long

We do not keep the technical data of visits. The service hosting the site keeps them only for as long as needed to deliver the pages and protect them from abuse, as described in its privacy policy, and then deletes them.

Your rights

You can ask at any time to access your data, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict its processing or to object to it, and for data portability (Articles 15 to 22 of the Regulation). Just write to the contact above.

If you think the processing is not lawful, you can lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (garanteprivacy.it).

Cookies

None, neither technical nor third-party. That is why the site does not ask for your consent to cookies.

When the service opens

When you can send floor plans and requests, we will update this page before the form starts collecting any data. You will find what we collect, why, for how long and who we share it with: for example with the partner installer in your area, only if you ask us to.
